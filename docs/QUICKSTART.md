# Quickstart

Your picture on a zoomable website of its own, in five steps. It's free, and it all happens in your web browser.

**You need** a GitHub account ([make one free](https://github.com/signup)) and a JPEG, PNG or TIFF of up to 25 MB.
Your picture will be public, so use one you have the right to publish.

1. Open [github.com/gjhilton/WhatIIIF](https://github.com/gjhilton/WhatIIIF). Click the green **Use this template**
   button, then **Create a new repository**. If GitHub asks you to sign in, do.

2. Type a name for your site in the **Repository name** box, such as `my-picture`. Check that **Public** is chosen.
   Click **Create repository**.

3. Click **Settings**, at the top of the page. Click **Pages**, on the left. Under **Source**, choose
   **GitHub Actions**.

4. Click **Code**, at the top of the page. Click the **content** folder. Click **Add file**, then **Upload files**.
   Drag your picture onto the page. Click **Commit changes**.

5. Wait three minutes. Click **Settings**, then **Pages**. Click **Visit site**. (No button yet? Wait a minute more,
   and reload the page.)

That's it: your picture is on the web.
